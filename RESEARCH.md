# Research notes

## 1. TH Rosenheim – Holztechnik (B.Eng.) curriculum

**Sources**

- *Studien- und Prüfungsordnung für den Bachelorstudiengang Holztechnik* (SPO, dated 14 Aug 2023). This is the current
  regulation and lists every module. <https://www.th-rosenheim.de/fileadmin/formalia/SPOs/HT/LF_HT_B_SPO_20242.pdf>
- *Modulhandbuch Bachelor Holztechnik* (status 22 Dec 2022, SPO 2015), 116 pages, with detailed module contents.
  <https://www.th-rosenheim.de/fileadmin/formalia/Studienplaene/20231/HTB_Modulhandbuch_20231.pdf>

**Key facts from the SPO (2023)**

- 7 semesters, Bachelor of Engineering; semester 5 is the practical semester (18 weeks in a company,
  plus accompanying lectures).
- **Vorpraxis (pre-study internship): at least 10 weeks**, normally before the programme starts and
  at the latest by the end of semester 3.
- Exams that must be taken by the end of semester 2: Baustatik & Festigkeitslehre, Ingenieurmathematik 1 & 2,
  Physik, Grundlagen der Bauphysik, Metallische Werkstoffe & Maschinenelemente.
- The bachelor's thesis can be written in English on request, but must include a German summary.
  Almost all teaching is in German.

The vocabulary in each subject was drawn from the "Inhalt" (content) sections of the module handbook.
Examples: Sorption, Fasersättigungspunkt and einheimische Holzarten (HT01); Kraftgrößenverfahren and
Eulersche Knickfälle (Statik); Schneidkeilgeometrie, Gleichlauf/Gegenlauf and Schneidstoffe
(Holzbearbeitungsmaschinen); Trocknungspläne, Vakuumtrocknung and Dämpfen (Holztrocknung); Adhäsion,
Kohäsion and Membranpresstechnik (Klebtechnik); BImSchG, TA Luft, the STOP principle and wood dust
(Umweltschutz); SPS, Feldbus, OPC UA and robotics (Automatisierung); Lean, OEE, SMED and value stream
mapping (Produktionsmanagement).

Two extra subjects cover language you need from day one but that isn't a module: **Studium & Hochschule**
(Klausur, Testat, Leistungspunkte, Vorpraxis …) and **Fachsprache** (berechnen, ermitteln, nachweisen,
bemessen; passive voice, nominal style, participle attributes, *sein + zu*, function-verb phrases).
These grammar patterns are the main gap between B2 general German and German engineering texts.

## 2. Similar apps and design takeaways

| App | What it does well | What we took |
|---|---|---|
| Duolingo | Fixed learning path, short rotating exercises, XP, streaks, crowns/levels | Path with unlocks, replay at higher difficulty, XP, streak, daily goal, varied exercise types |
| Memrise | Spaced repetition, listening and typing | Leitner-box review, dictation, typed answers |
| Anki | Strong SRS, but needs self-discipline and has little guidance | SRS runs automatically behind the guided path |
| Babbel / Busuu | Dialogue- and grammar-based lessons | Grammar drills and lecture listening with comprehension questions |
| GradGermany "Engineering German", German Academic Hub, "Technical German" audio courses | Generic engineering or academic vocabulary | Nothing wood-specific exists, which is the gap Holzwort fills |
